## Why West Richland Sees Storm Damage Different From Western Washington

West Richland sits on the high desert plateau of the Tri-Cities, where storm patterns look nothing like the steady drizzle on the coast. Summer brings fast-building thunderstorms that can produce straight-line winds and hail with little warning, and the surrounding basalt bedrock and clay-heavy soil don't absorb a sudden downpour the way sandier soils do.
