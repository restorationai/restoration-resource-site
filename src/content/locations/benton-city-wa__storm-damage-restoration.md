## Why Benton City Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Benton City sits in a high desert climate pocket along the Yakima River, where wide-open terrain and the Horse Heaven Hills funnel wind in ways that catch homeowners off guard. Gusts that build quickly during summer convective storms can shear roofing material, snap tree limbs onto carports and outbuildings, and drive wind-blown rain sideways under eaves that were never built to resist it. A lot of the housing stock in and around Benton City is older rural construction or manufactured homes on acreage, both of which tend to have lighter roof-to-wall connections than newer subdivisions closer to the Tri-Cities core. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Benton City

The first call is almost always about stopping ongoing intrusion, so our crews prioritize emergency tarping and board-up before anything else, especially on properties with exposed roof decking after a wind event. Debris removal, whether it's a fallen tree limb through a fence or hail-damaged gutters, gets handled alongside the drying work rather than left for a separate crew.
